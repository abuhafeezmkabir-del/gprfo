Our Mission

To make insurgency and persecution unprofitable.

To make insurgency and persecution unprofitable by:

1

Measuring It

Publish the Global Religious Freedom & Conflict Index, the definitive data source on attacks and risk.

  • LGA-level risk scores
  • Monthly incident reports
  • Trend analysis & forecasting
2

Predicting It

Operate GPRFO Risk Alerts — 30-day early warnings for governments and NGOs.

  • 30-day advance warning
  • Real-time risk escalations
  • Pattern recognition algorithms
3

Standardizing It

Maintain the GPRFO Protocol, the international standard for verifying persecution and coordinating response.

  • 3-layer verification system
  • Safe corridor data sharing
  • ICC-grade documentation standards
4

Protecting People

Give every aid worker and faith leader free tools to check if they're walking into danger.

  • GPRFO Safe alert system
  • WhatsApp risk-check line
  • Free for all humanitarian workers

Our Vision

The world we are building.

A world where no one is killed for their faith, and no village is burned without warning.

We envision a world where every government, NGO, and pastor can check the "GPRFO Risk Score" for a village before sending people there — the same way we check weather before flying.

In this world, insurgency is not just illegal. It's predictable and preventable.

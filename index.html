How GPRFO Works

A systematic approach to identifying, verifying, and responding to religious persecution.

Collect

Gather incident reports from local partners, media monitoring, and direct submissions from affected communities.

Verify

Apply rigorous 2-source rule and 3-layer verification to ensure accuracy before publication.

Distribute

Alert governments, NGOs, and communities in real-time to enable rapid response and protection.
